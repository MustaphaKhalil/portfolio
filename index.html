The problem

What I led

  • Capacity planning: modeled back-office queue impact ahead of launch and secured funding for two additional fraud-prevention hires before go-live.

The problem

Legacy POS hardware and its operating system were hitting end-of-life, creating a hard compliance deadline: miss it, and 300+ stores would carry serious PCI-compliance exposure and risk a full checkout outage across a multi-brand retail footprint. The system had to be replaced without a single day of store downtime.

What I led

  • Vendor strategy: weighed a custom headless build against an enterprise SaaS platform and chose the SaaS path trading some UI customization for a guaranteed, on-time migration ahead of the compliance deadline.
  • Business case: authored the funding case and presented the cost/risk trade-offs to the investment committee, securing $12.0M in Capex.
  • Requirements & identity: defined a unified single sign-on model for cross-brand sales and aligned backend teams on a standardized multi-brand store hierarchy.
  • Risk & test management: built isolated, rollback-enabled test environments to prevent hardware terminal burnout during integration testing, and ran weekly defect triage to clear high-priority and localization blockers.

Bell Mobility · Digital Customer Experience & Commerce Platforms · Feb 2022–Oct 2023

Mobile Number Transfer Automation

Letting customers port their number from a competitor without a help-desk call and without losing service the moment their new eSIM installs.

$1.51M
Cumulative operational savings from automated validation
$50.16M
Lifetime subscriber value protected
34.5K
Premium subscriptions saved annually from funnel abandonment
Role
Lead Project Manager
Audience
Mobile onboarding funnel digital, retail, call center & migrations
Capital
$4.95M Capex, Phase 1 portfolio
Focus
Legacy modernization & API orchestration

The problem

The number-porting flow was rigid and sequential: a competitor system timeout or a minor credential mismatch would hard-lock the transaction. Worse, if a number transfer started before a new eSIM profile finished installing, customers were left stranded with no service at all.

What I led

  • Orchestration redesign: re-engineered the system dependency path with a status-hook protocol that blocks a number port until the new eSIM profile is confirmed installed.
  • Backlog architecture: authored and managed 15 capabilities spanning front-end, API middleware, and real-time activation triggers across 11 engineering teams.
  • Dependency resolution: ran cross-team integration demos that caught and fixed critical database and API issues within two weeks, protecting the release timeline.
  • Capacity planning: modeled fallout-queue volume to keep support SLAs intact through launch.
