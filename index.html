In-Flight SIM Activation Program

Turning an in-cabin Wi-Fi partnership into a national customer-acquisition channel for international travelers and newcomers to Canada.

Role
Lead Product Manager, Portfolio Owner
Segment
International tourists & newcomers
Platform
Digital sales (web/app) & eSIM infrastructure
Scale
National, multi-brand rollout

The problem

Hundreds of thousands of international arrivals each year were a lucrative acquisition segment, but our standard digital activation flow needed 35 clicks, a Canadian credit card, and a local credit check — so competitors' airport kiosks won the moment of arrival instead of us.

What I led

  • Journey redesign: cut the activation flow from 35 clicks to 11 by analyzing drop-off data and redesigning the customer journey end to end.
  • New use cases: authored requirements for 7 new digital capabilities, including instant eSIM activation via IMEI/EID and international credit card acceptance.
  • Risk design: partnered with Fraud & Risk to bypass hard credit checks for newcomers while locking device financing for 180 days — approving more customers without adding bad debt.
  • Portfolio prioritization: won engineering capacity against 8 competing in-flight initiatives by presenting a data-driven ROI matrix to the executive steering committee.

CCRA Household Project

Modernizing credit-check & risk assessment so a residential customer adding a mobile line is treated as one trusted household — not a stranger.

Role
Lead Product Manager & Epic Owner
Segment
Existing residential customers adding mobility
Platforms
Enterprise retail CRM, billing & credit engines
Scale
National, multi-brand (Consumer & SMB)

The problem

To grow convergent "Mobility + Internet" households from 20% to 30%, retail reps needed a frictionless way to add a mobile line to an existing account — but every add-on triggered a full credit bureau pull, driving rep effort, drop-offs, and avoidable transaction fees.

What I led

  • Backlog architecture: authored and managed 15 capabilities across parallel workstreams — CRM UI, API gateway middleware, and credit-engine changes.
  • Segment prioritization: built a weighted ranking framework that shipped the highest-converting flows first and split Small Business from corporate accounts to protect developer velocity.
  • Security & compliance: led corporate sign-off on legacy-to-modern billing APIs and a secure customer-data tokenization protocol.
  • Capacity planning: modeled back-office queue impact ahead of launch and secured funding for two additional fraud-prevention hires before go-live.

Mobile Number Transfer Automation

Letting customers port their number from a competitor without a help-desk call — and without losing service the moment their new eSIM installs.

Role
Lead Product Manager, Portfolio Epic Owner
Audience
Mobile onboarding funnel — digital, retail & migrations
Capital
$4.95M Capex, Phase 1 portfolio
Focus
Legacy modernization & API orchestration

The problem

The number-porting flow was rigid and sequential: a competitor system timeout or a minor credential mismatch would hard-lock the transaction. Worse, if a number transfer started before a new eSIM profile finished installing, customers were left stranded with no service at all.

What I led

  • Orchestration redesign: re-engineered the system dependency path with a status-hook protocol that blocks a number port until the new eSIM profile is confirmed installed.
  • Backlog architecture: authored and managed 15 capabilities spanning front-end, API middleware, and real-time activation triggers across 11 engineering teams.
  • Dependency resolution: ran cross-team integration demos that caught and fixed critical database and API issues within two weeks, protecting the release timeline.
  • Capacity planning: modeled fallout-queue volume to keep support SLAs intact through launch.
